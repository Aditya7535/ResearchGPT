1. INTRODUCTION
Radiological diagnostic interpretation requires meticulous review of volumetric medical imaging. Deep vision-language transformers have emerged as a scalable method for preliminary automated screening. This paper evaluates multi-modal transformer performance on 12,000 chest CT scans annotated by board-certified radiologists.

2. METHODS
We collected 12,000 anonymized DICOM scans across three hospital networks. Scans were chunked by diagnostic region (Lung Parenchyma, Mediastinum, Bone Structure). Dense feature extraction utilized a 3D ResNet-Transformer backbone connected to a PubMed fine-tuned LLM.

3. RESULTS
The vision-language diagnostics engine achieved an overall AUC-ROC of 0.978 across all pathology classes. False positive rates were reduced by 34.2% compared to standalone convolutional baselines.

4. DISCUSSION
Automated transformer screening accelerates radiologist workflow triage significantly. Multi-site clinical trials are underway to validate real-time emergency department integration.
